Questions Before You Start

What to know about YS Calisthenics Academy before launch.

Who is YS Calisthenics Academy for?

YS Calisthenics Academy provides personalized training for all ages, with a special focus on helping beginners build a strong foundation. Training can be adapted to each person's current ability.

Do I need previous calisthenics experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Students start with the fundamentals and progress at their own pace while learning proper movement patterns and building confidence.

What is the goal of the academy?

The goal is to create a fun and supportive community where people can build strength together, learn real calisthenics skills, and develop confidence and discipline through training.

What makes the academy different?

Students learn real calisthenics skills from an experienced trainer while being part of a supportive community. Training is about learning body control, improving consistently, and enjoying the process.

What does a typical session look like?

Sessions begin with a warm-up, move into strength and conditioning work, then focus on calisthenics movements and skills with personalized guidance. Games, challenges, and flexible practice may be included before a cooldown.

What equipment is needed?

Training is mostly bodyweight-based, with some equipment provided when needed. Students can begin without needing a collection of expensive equipment.

How are classes organized?

Classes are held as a mixed group where everyone trains together while receiving personalized guidance. Exercises and progressions are adjusted based on experience, strength, and goals.

Is this only for advanced athletes?

No. The academy is especially welcoming to beginners. Advanced skills are taught through proper progressions, and students are never expected to perform difficult movements before they are ready.

Is training safe for beginners?

Safety is a priority. Students learn the fundamentals first, build strength gradually, learn proper technique, and practice how to safely bail from skills when appropriate.

Where are classes held?

YS Calisthenics Academy classes will be held in Irving, Texas. Specific schedules will be shared as launch approaches.

How can I join?

During the pre-launch period, you can email yahyasaleem700@gmail.com or send a DM to @ys_cali. A registration/interest form will be added when it is finalized.
