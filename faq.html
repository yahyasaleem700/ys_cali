Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YS_CALI training.

FAQ

Do I need calisthenics experience?

No. Training can be adapted for beginners, intermediate athletes, and advanced students.

What do we train?

We focus on strength foundations, mobility, body control, and progressing toward calisthenics skills.

Do I need equipment?

Most training uses bodyweight movements. Additional equipment may be used as students progress.

How do I join?

Reach out through Instagram or email to learn about upcoming sessions.

Is this only for advanced athletes?

No. The goal is to help anyone improve, regardless of their starting point.

What makes YS Calisthenics Academy different?

The focus is community. Students train together, encourage each other, and grow together.

Still Have Questions?

Feel free to reach out and ask.
