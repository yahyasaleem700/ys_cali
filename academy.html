Our Mission

YS Calisthenics Academy exists to bring people together through movement. Our goal is to create an environment where students of all levels can improve their strength, learn new skills, and support each other's progress.

What We Focus On

Strength Foundations

Building the basics: push-ups, pull-ups, dips, squats, and core strength.

Mobility & Control

Developing flexibility, balance, and body awareness needed for advanced skills.

Calisthenics Skills

Progressing toward skills such as handstands, levers, and other bodyweight movements.

Community

Training together, encouraging each other, and creating a positive environment.

Who Is It For?

Beginners

No previous experience required. Learn the foundations safely.

Intermediate Athletes

Improve strength, technique, and unlock new skills.

Advanced Athletes

Continue progressing and refine higher-level movements.
