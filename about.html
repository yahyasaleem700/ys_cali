About YS_CALI

Strength is built through consistency, discipline, and a commitment to improvement.

My Journey

Calisthenics has been a journey of learning what the human body is capable of. Through years of training, I have focused on developing strength, control, and mastery over bodyweight movements.

YS_CALI was created to share that journey and help others discover their own potential through calisthenics.

Training Philosophy

Discipline

Progress comes from showing up consistently and putting in the work.

Strength & Control

Calisthenics is about more than muscle. It is about control, coordination, and skill.

Helping Others

The goal is to create an environment where people support each other and improve together.

Skills & Goals

Current focus includes skills such as handstands, back levers, and human flags while continuing to develop strength and athletic ability.
