ABOUT YS_CALI

The Journey Behind the Training

Strength, control, and a commitment to helping others improve.

THE JOURNEY

Why I Train

Calisthenics has been a journey of learning what the body is capable of. Through consistent training, the focus has grown beyond individual skills into strength, control, patience, and discipline.

ys_cali was created to share that mindset and help other people discover what they can build through bodyweight training.

PHILOSOPHY

How I Approach Training

01

Build the Basics

Strong fundamentals create the foundation for harder skills.

02

Progress with Control

Move deliberately, improve technique, and earn the next progression.

03

Grow Together

Training is more meaningful when people encourage and challenge each other.

TRAINING & SAFETY

Progress Comes Before Complexity

Advanced skills are not rushed. Training emphasizes strengthening the basics, learning proper technique, progressing gradually, and learning how to safely bail from skills when needed.
